    你究竟是先把字典背下来，把每一个意思都记住，才叫会语文了吗？那么英语学习，一定需要记住每一个单词的每一个含义吗？

    长难句

    高中学的那些东西，连接词，主从句等（就是语法）

    再思考一个事情：

    为什么要学语法划分长难句，有什么意义？
    • 语法是工具，结合中文，主谓宾，定状补，我们说话看书不会去纠结这些，看书也不会，但如果句子太长了读不懂（中文有个好例子：鲁迅的文章）就可以使用这个划分句子，见难则用之。
    1. Now the nation’s top patent court appears completely ready to scale back on business-method patents, which have been controversial ever since they were first authorized 10 years ago.

    scale back 缩减

    最后authorized是被授权，联系上下文得知是围绕专利展开，所以authorized对象是patent,指那些被授权的专利。

    1. Curbs on business-method claims would be a dramatic about-face, because it was the Federal Circuit itself that introduced such patents with its 1998 decision in the so-called State Street Bank case, approving a patent on a way of pooling mutual-fund assets.

    curb 控制，限制 claim 声明 about-face 转变，转身 the Federal Circuit 联邦巡回法庭 pooling 聚集

    it is/was …that… 强调句型（区分从句）so-called 所谓的

    限制发生了转变，因为他们引入了专利。

    1. In his book The Tipping Point, Malcolm Gladwell argues that “social epidemics” are driven in large part by the actions of a tiny minority of special individuals, often called influentials, who are unusually informed, persuasive, or well connected.

    …that “social epidemics” are driven in large part by the actions of a tiny minority of special individuals, often called influentials, who are unusually informed, persuasive, or well connected.

    这里unusually表示非常！！

    1. The Federal Circuit issued an unusual order stating that the case would be heard by all 12 of the court’s judges, rather than a typical panel of three,and one issue it wants to evaluate is whether it should “reconsider” its State Street Bank ruling.

    …stating that the case would be heard by all 12 of the court’s judges, rather than a typical panel of three, and that one issue it wants to evaluate is whether it should “reconsider” its State Street Bank ruling.

    judge 法官 panel 小组

    并列的一定是词性相同的！！！！按动词也可以划分句子
